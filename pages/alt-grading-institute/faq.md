<dl>
<dt> What is the primary goal of the Institute?</dt>
<dd>To provide an intensive, supportive environment in which participants will design or refine an alternative grading scheme aligned with their values and course context.</dd>

<dt>Who should apply?</dt>
<dd>Faculty who have either experimented with alternative grading or are ready to commit to making a change. This is not an introductory “why grading matters” workshop—it’s for people who want to operationalize their values.</dd>

<dt>How much time should I expect to commit?</dt>
<dd>Pre-work: ~2–3 hours (readiness assessment, short readings, reflection).<br/>
Institute: Two full programming days (approx. 5–6 hours each, designed to accommodate multiple time zones).<br/>
Optional: Continued engagement with peers and facilitators afterward.</dd>

<dt>What kinds of pre-work will I do?</dt>
<dd>Readings on grading history and practices, completing a readiness self-assessment, and preparing to share a syllabus or course outline.</dd>

<dt>How are participants selected?</dt>
<dd>Selection balances clarity of goals, alignment with alternative grading practices, and diversity across institutions, disciplines, course types, and student populations.</dd>

<dt>What if I’m new to alternative grading?</dt>
<dd>That’s fine! You don’t need prior experience, just a willingness to learn and revise your grading scheme.</dd>

<dt>Is the Institute online or in person?</dt>
<dd>The 2025 Institute will be fully online. Future years may rotate between online and in-person formats.</dd>
